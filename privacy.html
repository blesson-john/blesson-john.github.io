Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Benny Bot is a private app used only by the members of one household. It is not available to the public.

Google data it uses

  • Gmail (read-only): Benny Bot reads emails that look like bills or statements to find the payee, amount and due date. It cannot send, change or delete email.
  • Google Calendar: Benny Bot reads and creates events on the household's calendars when a household member asks it to.

How the data is handled

  • Data is stored on a computer owned by the household.
  • Message and email text may be sent to the Anthropic Claude API to understand requests and pull out bill details. Anthropic does not use API data to train its models by default.
  • Data is never sold, shared for advertising, or given to anyone outside the household, except the service providers needed to run the app (Google, Anthropic, Meta WhatsApp and Notion).
  • Benny Bot's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Stored data is deleted when the app is removed.

Contact

Contact the household administrator using the support email on the Google sign-in screen.
